Step: 1. Define the user stories based on the Email Router product spec.

Result:
As a Customer Support Representative, I want an Email Ingestion System to seamlessly integrate with email services so that I can reduce my workload on routine inquiries and focus on complex issues.
As a Subject Matter Expert (SME), I want a Message Classification Module to analyze email content and determine intent so that I receive only relevant, complex inquiries, improving efficiency and job satisfaction.
As an IT Administrator, I want a User Interface with a dashboard for monitoring system performance so that I can be responsible for system configuration, maintenance, and monitoring performance metrics.
As a Customer Support Representative, I want a Response Generation Engine to generate contextually accurate responses so that I can provide accurate and consistent information to customers.
As a Subject Matter Expert (SME), I want Routing Logic to assign emails to appropriate SMEs based on content analysis so that I can efficiently handle complex inquiries in my area of expertise.
As a Customer Support Representative, I want a Knowledge Base Integration to efficiently store and retrieve organizational knowledge so that I can access relevant information for responding to inquiries.
As an IT Administrator, I want the system to comply with data protection regulations so that sensitive information is handled appropriately and securely.
As a Customer Support Representative, I want the system to process and classify incoming emails within 5 seconds of receipt so that I can respond promptly to customer inquiries.
As an IT Administrator, I want the system to maintain 99.9% uptime so that continuous email processing capabilities are ensured.
As a Customer Support Representative, I want the system to handle a minimum of 10,000 emails per hour without degradation in performance so that I can efficiently manage high email volumes during peak periods.
