Step: 1. Define the user stories based on the Email Router product spec.

Result:
User Story 1:
Role: Customer Support Representative
Goal: Automatically categorize and prioritize incoming emails for efficient handling of complex issues.

User Story 2:
Role: Subject Matter Expert
Goal: Receive only relevant and complex inquiries routed for specialized assistance.

User Story 3:
Role: IT Administrator
Goal: Monitor system performance and manage knowledge base settings through a dashboard for Email Router operation.

User Story 4:
Role: System User
Goal: Have the option to manually intervene in email routing for critical communications control.
